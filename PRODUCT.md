# GoFish

**register:** product

## Product purpose

Orca's browser Target tool, pulled out of Orca and into Chrome/Brave as a side panel. Cast into
the page, hook an element, and the context an agent needs lands on the clipboard in Orca's own
format. It exists because Orca's embedded browser costs screen real estate the portrait monitor
doesn't have.

## Users

One person: a design-engineer running Orca on a right-hand monitor, the browser on a centre
monitor, reviewing a localhost UI. Uses the panel in three-second bursts — cast, click, paste —
dozens of times an hour. Not a dashboard to sit in.

## Scene

Daylight desk. The panel is welded to the edge of a bright browser window, inches from the page
being judged. It must never bias the colours of the page it sits next to, and must not look like
a dark terminal bolted onto a light browser.

## Tone

Terse, physical, confident. Fishing verbs because they're the real metaphor for what the tool
does, never a mascot, never an emoji.

## Anti-references

- Dark terminal dev-tool (neon on black, monospace everywhere).
- Linear/Vercel graphite + violet SaaS panel.
- Cards in a grid, icon + heading + body.
- Cute fish illustrations, aquarium blues.

## Principles

1. The panel must not contaminate colour judgment of the page beside it.
2. One keystroke-sized action is the whole product: Cast.
3. Everything captured is disposable. Clearing costs nothing, so never ask twice.
